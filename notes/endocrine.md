# The Endocrine System

## Glands
The pituitary gland is often called the master gland because it controls other glands. The thyroid releases thyroxine, which regulates metabolism. The adrenal glands release cortisol, which helps the body respond to stress, and adrenaline, which prepares the body for fight or flight. The pancreas releases insulin and glucagon. The gonads produce sex hormones.

## Blood glucose control
After a meal, blood glucose rises and the pancreas releases insulin, which helps cells take up glucose and the liver store it as glycogen. When blood glucose falls, the pancreas releases glucagon, which makes the liver break glycogen down into glucose. In type 1 diabetes, the insulin-producing beta cells of the pancreas are destroyed.

## Feedback
Most hormones are controlled by negative feedback: a rise in a hormone or its effect triggers a response that reduces further release. The hypothalamus and pituitary gland coordinate many of these loops.
