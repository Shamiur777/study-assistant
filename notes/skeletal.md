# The Skeletal System

## Overview
An adult human skeleton has 206 bones. The axial skeleton includes the skull, vertebral column and rib cage. The appendicular skeleton includes the limbs and the girdles that attach them. Bones support the body, protect organs, allow movement, store calcium and phosphorus, and produce blood cells in red bone marrow.

## Bone types
Bones are classified by shape. Long bones such as the femur are longer than they are wide. Short bones such as the carpals are roughly cube-shaped. Flat bones such as the skull bones and sternum protect organs. Irregular bones such as the vertebrae have complex shapes, and sesamoid bones such as the patella develop inside tendons.

## Joints
Fibrous joints, like the sutures of the skull, do not move. Cartilaginous joints, like those between vertebrae, allow limited movement. Synovial joints allow free movement and contain lubricating synovial fluid. A hinge joint, such as the elbow, moves in one plane, while a ball-and-socket joint, such as the hip and shoulder, allows movement in many directions.
