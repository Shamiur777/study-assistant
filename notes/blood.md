# Blood

## Components
Plasma makes up about 55 percent of blood and carries water, nutrients, hormones and waste. Red blood cells carry oxygen using the protein hemoglobin. White blood cells defend the body against infection. Platelets are cell fragments that help blood clot.

## Blood groups
The ABO system classifies blood as A, B, AB or O based on antigens on red blood cells. The Rh factor adds a positive or negative label. People with type O negative blood are called universal donors for red cells, and people with AB positive blood are universal recipients.

## Clotting
When a vessel is damaged, platelets stick together to form a plug. A series of clotting factors then converts the soluble protein fibrinogen into insoluble fibrin threads, which form a mesh that traps cells and seals the wound.
