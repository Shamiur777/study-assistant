# The Muscular System

## Muscle types
Skeletal muscle is voluntary and striated, and it moves the bones. Cardiac muscle is found only in the heart; it is striated but involuntary. Smooth muscle is involuntary and non-striated, and it lines organs such as the intestines and blood vessels.

## Contraction
Muscle fibres contain filaments of actin and myosin. In the sliding filament model, myosin heads attach to actin and pull the filaments past each other, shortening the sarcomere. This process needs calcium ions released inside the cell and energy from ATP.

## Antagonistic pairs
Muscles can only pull, so they work in pairs. When the biceps contracts the elbow bends, and the triceps relaxes. To straighten the arm, the triceps contracts and the biceps relaxes. Tendons attach muscles to bones.
