# The Lungs and Breathing

## Structure
Air travels down the trachea, which divides into two bronchi, one for each lung. The bronchi branch into smaller bronchioles that end in tiny air sacs called alveoli. The right lung has three lobes and the left lung has two lobes, leaving room for the heart.

## Gas exchange
Alveoli have very thin walls and are surrounded by capillaries. Oxygen diffuses from the air in the alveoli into the blood, and carbon dioxide diffuses from the blood into the alveoli to be breathed out. The huge number of alveoli gives a large surface area for this exchange.

## Breathing mechanics
To inhale, the diaphragm contracts and flattens while the intercostal muscles lift the ribs. This increases the volume of the chest cavity, lowers the pressure inside, and air flows in. At rest, exhalation is passive: the diaphragm relaxes, chest volume decreases and air is pushed out.
