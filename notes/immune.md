# The Immune System

## Innate defences
The first line of defence is physical and chemical barriers such as skin, mucus and stomach acid. If pathogens get past these, innate immune cells such as neutrophils and macrophages engulf them by phagocytosis. Inflammation and fever are also part of the innate response.

## Adaptive immunity
Adaptive immunity is slower but specific. B cells produce antibodies that bind to particular antigens. Helper T cells coordinate the response, and cytotoxic T cells destroy infected cells. After an infection, memory cells remain and allow a faster response on re-exposure.

## Vaccination
A vaccine exposes the immune system to a harmless form of an antigen. The body makes antibodies and memory cells without causing the disease, so it can respond quickly if the real pathogen appears later.
